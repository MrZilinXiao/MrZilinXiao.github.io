Greetings!

Zilin Xiao received his CS PhD at Rice University, where he was fortunately advised by Prof. Vicente Ordonez.

His research broadly focuses on vision-language models, large language models, and their reasoning & embedding capabilities -- especially how reasoning and retrieval can be mutually beneficial for each other.

His recent research interests lie in:

  • Vision-Language Models (VLMs): grounding, alignment, and reliable multimodal reasoning
  • Large Language Models (LLMs): post-training for reasoning-centric models
  • Embeddings & Retrieval: retrieval, re-ranking and representation learning
